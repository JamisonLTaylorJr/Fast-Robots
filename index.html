THE ENGINEER

About me

I'm Jamison L. Taylor Jr., a Master of Engineering student in Mechanical Engineering at Cornell University, focusing on mechanical design and robotics. My interests lie at the intersection of mechanical systems, mechatronics, embedded programming, and feedback control.

This portfolio documents my work in MAE 5190: Fast Robots, a hands-on course focused on the design and development of autonomous mobile robots. The labs cover the progression from embedded communication and sensor integration to control, mapping, localization, and path planning.
